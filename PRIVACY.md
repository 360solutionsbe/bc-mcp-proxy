# Privacy Policy

The privacy policy for the Business Central MCP Proxy (`vgs-bc-mcp`, including the Claude Desktop extension bundle) is published at:

**https://vangeldersolutions.github.io/bc-mcp-proxy/privacy/**

The source of that page is [`docs/privacy.md`](docs/privacy.md) in this repository; edit it there so the published page and the repository stay identical.

In short: the proxy runs on your own device, talks only to Microsoft Entra ID and your Business Central environment, keeps tokens and a tools cache locally, collects no telemetry, and sends nothing to Vangelder Solutions. Business Central data that reaches your AI client is governed by that provider's policy and your subscription with it — see [`NOTICE.md`](NOTICE.md).
